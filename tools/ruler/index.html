Online Ruler

An online ruler in actual size — measure in cm, mm and inches right on the screen, and calibrate it with a bank card so the millimetres are real.

What is this? Hide details ▾

What is Online Ruler?

A ruler drawn straight onto your screen, with centimetres and millimetres along one edge and inches down to 1/16 along the other. No browser can know how big your display really is, so you calibrate once by matching an outline to a bank card — or by typing a length you already know — and from then on the marks are true to size. Drag the two markers to measure anything you can lay on the glass; it works offline and remembers the calibration on this device.
