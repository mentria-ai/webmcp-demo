Step Counter

Count your steps on a walk, right in the browser. Uses your phone's motion sensor — nothing is uploaded.

What is this? Hide details ▾

What is Step Counter?

A session-based pedometer that reads your phone's accelerometer through the DeviceMotion API and counts steps with on-device peak detection. Start a session and walk with the screen on — it tracks steps, distance, pace, and a daily goal ring. All processing stays in the browser: no account, no upload, no background tracking. Desktops and devices without a motion sensor show a disabled state. Daily totals persist on this device, with a 7-day chart and goal streaks. Pocket mode keeps the screen on but pure black (best on OLED) so steps keep counting in your pocket; short locked-screen gaps are filled from your walking pace and labeled as estimates.
