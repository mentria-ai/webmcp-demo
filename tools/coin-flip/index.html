What is Coin Flip?

A no-frills coin flip backed by the browser's secure RNG (Web Crypto). Heads or tails is decided locally; nothing is logged or sent to a server. Keeps a running history per session. Useful for quick decisions, fair starts, or settling tabletop arguments — instant on every device.
