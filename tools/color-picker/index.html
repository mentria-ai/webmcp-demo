What is Color Picker?

Pick, sample, and save colors entirely in your browser. Outputs HEX, RGB, and HSL side by side. Saved swatches persist via localStorage; nothing is uploaded. Click into the picker, copy any value to clipboard, or build a small palette for design or code. Works offline.
