Decibel Meter

A sound level meter in your browser — measure noise in dB with your phone or laptop mic, no app to install.

What is this? Hide details ▾

What is Decibel Meter?

Point your phone or laptop mic at whatever is making noise and this reads the level in decibels, live — with a session min, average and peak, plus a ladder of everyday sounds for context. Phone microphones aren't calibrated instruments, so the numbers are good estimates for comparing rooms, not legal measurements. Nothing is recorded: the sound is analysed on your device, frame by frame, and never leaves it.

Your mic reports a relative level, not real-world decibels. This offset converts one into the other; 90 is a sensible starting point for most phones and laptops. If you have a reference meter nearby, nudge the slider until the two numbers agree.

The screen stays awake while the meter is running.

Measure the sound around you

This uses your microphone to read the sound level in real time. Mic processing like noise suppression is switched off, so the meter hears the room as it really is.

Microphone access blocked

Allow microphone access for this site, then try again. On iPhone and iPad, tap the AA button in the address bar and allow the microphone, or check Settings › Safari › Microphone. On a computer, click the padlock next to the address and turn the microphone on.

Microphone not available

This browser doesn't give web pages access to microphone input. Try the latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

Secure connection needed

Browsers only hand over the microphone over HTTPS. Open this tool on the live site (https://webmcp.mentria.ai). A local dev server reached by LAN IP over plain HTTP cannot open the mic.
